https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/pull/15233 changed the format of `EventContext` and as such required two small hacks to make the serialization backwards compatible:

https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/553f2f53e7930d721d2070ffa45af6397f5ecb60/synapse/events/snapshot.py#L206-L212

https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/553f2f53e7930d721d2070ffa45af6397f5ecb60/synapse/events/snapshot.py#L189-L191

We should remove these after a few (let's say 4?) releases. The second one (lines 189-191) can just be removed, and the first one just requires a change:
```
state_group_deltas = _decode_state_group_delta(input["state_group_deltas"])
```
and the removal of the `if` statement.
