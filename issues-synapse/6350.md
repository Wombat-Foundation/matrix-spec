Related to #6348.

I've stumbled over the following in your last blog post:

> please also remember to turn off public registration on your homeserver. Otherwise even with
the changes above, if randoms can sign up on your server to view & join rooms then all bets are
off.

Please urgently make that setting disabled by default in synapse, otherwise you'll end up with an ecosystem of abandoned home servers used by spammers. BTDT in the XMPP world.

Ideally, a server admin should have a way to hand out registration tokens to potential users, or perform registration on their behalf. Otherwise, homeserver admins will be inclined to just enable self-registration and the final effect will be the same.

Number of XMPP servers I've received spam from in the last 14 days: 104.
