the `matrix.org` deployment of synapse has its own copies of many of the templates, and, as a general rule, they are *worse* than the defaults.

Apart from making things uglier, it also tends to mean people consider the verification emails as spam.

We should check how the overrides differ from the defaults, and remove the overrides where possible.
