There isn't necessarily an error with the config; the problem is with your installation.

For example:

https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/3503f42741d438b67490bc774ee2c3a856f6bc81/synapse/config/tracer.py#L43-L48
https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/493c2fc44abcf3457953cc2f6f23509ff7855253/synapse/config/jwt.py#L45-L50

grep for `except (ImportError|Dependency).*:\n.*raise` to find all of these.

Instead, let `check_requirements` raise a `DependencyException`.
