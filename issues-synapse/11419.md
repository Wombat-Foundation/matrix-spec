**Description:**

from reading https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/24b61f379ac1fc740e1b569b85363e2a0411883a/docs/admin_api/user_admin_api.md#deactivate-account deactivations currently function akin to an account deletion. it would be beneficial to offer a way to simply prevent a user logging in until further notice, with an additional endpoint to undo this. semantically I'd rename the current API to "account deletion" and introduce a new system to flag an account as unloginable (deactivated), but you could also just introduce a new endpoint for this functionality called "account freeze" or similar

motivation for such a feature is sometimes you need to react fast to *potential* incidents and stop further actions but you'd hedge your bets if the choices are either "leave the account usable" or "permanently delete the account". this is very much also the case if you're looking in to automated abuse mitigation which will sometimes get the call wrong
