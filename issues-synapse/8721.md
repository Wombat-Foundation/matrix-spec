A host's saml2 metadata recently expired (as seen by the `validUntil` attribute on the metadata):
```
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8" standalone="no"?>
<md:EntityDescriptor xmlns:md="urn:oasis:names:tc:SAML:2.0:metadata" entityID="https://accounts.google.com/o/saml2?idpid=foo" validUntil="2020-10-17T22:17:45.000Z">
```
