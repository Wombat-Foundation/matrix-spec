**Description:**

<!-- Describe here the feature you are requesting. -->
In the EU a service provider processing and storing person related data is enforced by law of DSGVO to provide a declaration of privacy protection (in german: Datenschutzerklärung). Under certain circumstances a user even has to actively agree to them and as a provider you sometimes have to prove that consent.

To make this possible synapse would have to provide an REST endpoint where such a declaration customized by the provider of the homeserver could be stored. Then a matrix client like element would be able to query and display it to the end user and also aks for consent. That consent could then be stored by synapse.
