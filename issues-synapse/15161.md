### Description

https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/pull/12132 introduced functionality to spread out the federation transmission requests due to device-list updates. However it also applies to to-device messages (due to the default `immediate=False` at https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/pull/12132/files#diff-4db75239ae91da3be91b6ccacb4dc969be778e832da2976b9b8fd628f16e6b52R674)  which doesn't *appear* to have been the intention, and can cause significant delays in transmission of to-device messages.

### Steps to reproduce

- log in on a new device
- soon after, send an E2E message
- express sadness at how long the keys take to turn up.
