### Description

The option to add an email to his account, for which Riot (for example) does not ask for confirmation of the actual password, is a huge security breach. Suppose your session is open on a public computer or someone got access to your computer, anyone can add an email address, confirm it and then change your password and do what he wants. I reported it on #riot:matrix.org and #riot-android:matrix.org in the first place, but they suggested that I report it here.

### Steps to reproduce

1. Leave a Matrix session open in your browser (note that you do not know the current password of the user)
2. Use the "add email address" option in your client (Riot support it), add the email and confirm it.
3. Open https://riot.im/app/#/forgot_password and fill the fields.
4. Confirm the link that was sent.
5. You can now access to the matrix account with the new password.
