Summary:
When launching Synapse it fails to check that the configured certificate hasn't expired and will launch with the expired certificate. (Causing clients to just stall with a spinner upon trying to login.)

Steps to Reproduce:
Let the configured certificate for synapse expire. (Sometimes your Let's Encrypt automation fails…) Restart synapse.

Expected Results:
Synapse should either refuse to launch or at the very least, loudly complain in the logs that it is launching with an expired (or otherwise invalid) certificate which causes client to silently fail at logging in.

Actual Results:
Synapse just launches, ignoring the fact that clients won't be able to connect.

Regression:
n/a

Notes:
Synapse 0.22.1, FreeBSD 11.0p11 (though the OS is irrelevant to this issue.)
