https://matrix-org.github.io/synapse/latest/sso_mapping_providers.html mentions that OIDC mapping providers should be configured via `oidc_config.user_mapping_provider` but https://matrix-org.github.io/synapse/latest/usage/configuration/config_documentation.html#oidc_providers says `oidc_config` is deprecated and should be migrated over to the `oidc_providers` format.
