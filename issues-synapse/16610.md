**Description:**

Currently going by Element's room controls there are no room controls on the Server side in place to prevent someone from spamming links (that trigger image previews) or images of a less than ideal nature. For example, CSAM or other material not desirable can be easily spammed. Other social software, like Discourse and others have moderation tools that prevent newly created accounts from engaging in this kind of spam.

<!-- Describe here the feature you are requesting. -->

**Feature Request:**

Two entries are added to "Permissions" alongside existing entries like "Send Reactions" "Send Messages":

- "Send Files/Images"
- "Send Links"

This puts in place simple preventative controls so that moderators aren't having to spend time with reactive based ones and room members aren't exposed to problematic material
