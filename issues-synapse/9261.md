**Description:**
With the amount of users growing on the matrix fediverse, people more and more receive unwanted invitations for groups or direct messages. 
**Possible approach:**
To approach this problem, users should get additional settings to automatically reject invitations for groups and, if wished, for direct messages. The inviter should be informed about the automatic rejection.
**Useful side effect:**
At the same time, the inviter's homeserver could monitor the failed inviting attempts to offer a measurement for fighting spam.
