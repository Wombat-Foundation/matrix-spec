[MSC2229](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-doc/blob/anoa/rebind_3pids/proposals/2229-rebind-existing-3pid.md) describes changes to:

* `POST /_matrix/client/r0/account/3pid/email/requestToken`
* `POST /_matrix/client/r0/account/3pid/msisdn/requestToken`

allowing 3PID owners to rebind 3PIDs already on their HS account.
