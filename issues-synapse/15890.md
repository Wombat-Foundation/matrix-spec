### Description

According to https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/release-v1.87/synapse/handlers/deactivate_account.py#L134-L137 email pushers should not be associated with devices.

Rational : mail notifications should continue to be sent even after the device that set up the pusher has been deleted (ie: after a logout, for example).

But https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/release-v1.87/synapse/handlers/register.py#L1010-L1017 track device id at user registration,

and https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/release-v1.87/synapse/push/pusherpool.py#L97-L190 do not filter pusher kind (ie: "email") to decide whether or not to tracks device id.

### Steps to reproduce

- add an email address
- setup email notif
- logout
- login
- => email notif are disable
