Perhaps relevant is is a rejected pull-request: https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/pull/4609
I think it is not sufficient that the key is fetched https, especially in light of the recent security incident. I have no problem comparing a simple fingerprint and in fact, I consider the absence of a fingerprint, not the presence, off-putting for (new) users.
