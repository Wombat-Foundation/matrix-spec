I upgraded a room from matrix.org and the bridge (which lives on gnome.org) inside the room tried to handle the upgrade (it does this by trying to join the `content.replacement_room` as soon as it sees the upgrade event). The bridge tried to join `!HbIRFiVYbAwQBbobIc:matrix.org` and got a response of `{\"errcode\":\"M_UNKNOWN\",\"error\":\"Failed to make_join via any server\"}`.

Given this is a fresh room, I wouldn't have expected the room join to time out. The error seems to be a result of the remote side failing rather than gnome.org being too slow, so I am curious as to how this happened.
