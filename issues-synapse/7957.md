We seem to have 12 client reader workers on matrix.org atm, and each of them is doing ~10-30Hz of get_relations traffic (triggered by calls to /messages). However, we have a cache hit rate of 10-40%, and the cache size is only 1000 items(!).  Surely we should have a much bigger cache size, to avoid hammering the DB with requests to look for aggregations.
