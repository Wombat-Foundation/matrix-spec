I'd like to enable/disable federation for users based on their LDAP identity or more detailed e.g. on groups they are assigned to in LDAP. 

In a first step it would be very nice to have some kind of a whitelist/blacklist allowing/disallowing users from using the federation feature analog to whitelist/blacklisted servers.
