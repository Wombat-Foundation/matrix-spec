- OS: Debian Jessie
- Synapse version: 0.18.7-1
- Python2 version: 2.7.9
- Python3 version: 3.4.2


On my synapse server, a user first registered with a very simple password based on regular ASCII characters. She could log in and use Riot without any issue.

Later, this user changer her password to something much longer, _and with special characters, such as 'ê'._

The password change worked. However, after doing that, she could not log in anymore with her account (with the new password), and faced an error "Internal Error 500" (or something like that) on Riot on all her devices.

Looking at the logs, I discovered that the problem seemed to be on Synapse's side: each time she tried to log in with her new password containing special chars, this happened in the logs:
