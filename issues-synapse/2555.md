### Description

Impossible to rename a room or to invite members in a private 1to1 room (members and room are hosted on my Synapse server).
It seemed to happen after iOS/Android app update.

Context: I have my own synapse server 0.23.1-1 on Debian Stretch, there are only 3 account on this server (me, friendA, friendB) and I have 2 rooms A and B where I am discussing on 1to1 with friendA and friendB.

Before the last update: the room were named with the name of each friend (room A is named friendA and room B is named friendB).

After the last update: in iOS the room B has been renamed friendA (but room A remains with the name friendA, so I have 2 rooms named the same) and the member list of room B is listing friendA as a member instead of friendB. friendB cannot post in the room B anymore.
However on riot-web, the room B name and room B member list hasn't changed.
Either with riot-ios or riot-web, I cannot rename the room or change the member list because I got an "Internal Server Error".
