The 3pid-invite version of `POST /_matrix/client/v3/rooms/{roomId}/invite` ([spec](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.3/client-server-api/#post_matrixclientv3roomsroomidinvite-1)) has `id_access_token` as a required parameter.

We currently accept requests without an `id_access_token`, and then call `/_matrix/identity/api/v1/lookup`, which was removed from the spec over a year ago (https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/2713).

Instead, we should simply reject any requests that do not pass an `id_access_token`.
