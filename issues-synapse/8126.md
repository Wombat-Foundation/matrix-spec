### Description

`!:sorunome.de` is an existing room (made as an experiment using a patched Synapse). [MSC 1597](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-doc/blob/master/proposals/1597-id-grammar.md) specifies

> The opaque_id part must not be empty, and must consist entirely of the characters `[0-9a-zA-Z.=_-]`.
