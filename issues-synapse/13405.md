### Description

Some logs:

```
synapse    | 2022-07-27 18:49:44,340 - synapse.http.client - 446 - INFO - as-sender-telegram-109 - Received response to PUT [http://mtele:29317/transactions/1127?access_token=<redacted>](http://mtele:29317/transactions/1127?access_token=%3Credacted%3E): 200
synapse    | 2022-07-27 18:51:23,608 - synapse.http.client - 180 - INFO - sentinel - Dropped 127.0.0.2 from DNS resolution to [matrix.myownwebserver.mooo.com](http://matrix.myownwebserver.mooo.com/) due to blacklist
```

Story behind those logs:

I have a docker-compose installation, synapse, postgres, and mautrix-telegram.
Synaps needs to communicate with mautrix-telegram. and by chance, docker-compose gave the mautrix-telegram the IP address of 127.0.0.2.

By chance, some dude that got his domain blacklisted, used that domain to resolve a machine on his internal network.
- synapse isn't working for that dude as he's blacklisted
- all people with setups like me can't get synapse to connect to internal-tools/services

### Steps to reproduce

- docker-compose with synapse, postgres, mautrix-telegram (or any service that synapse has to connect to via HTTP)
- enable federation
- let it run for 15 minutes until the blacklist is updated
- a lot of domains in the blacklist resolve to IP in 127.0.0.0/24 range
- your docker-compose internal network probably have the same range
- your synapse starts reporting DNSLookupFailed when trying to connect to your own services
