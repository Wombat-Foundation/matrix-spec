A Riot instance that had been logged in as `@lb:ldbco.de` for a while (on a laptop that was slept/shutdown a few times) seemed to freeze on last Wednesday. For about a week after Wednesday, the latest messages in each room appeared to have been received by my HS before Thurs Mar 09 2017 (Wednesday and earlier).  Back/forward pagination was working, but the latest events that could be loaded occurred on Wednesday. 

The sync process of the frozen client had logs that looked like
`2017-03-15T20:24:05.700Z I MatrixClient sync state => %s RECONNECTING`
`2017-03-15T20:24:17.370Z E /sync error %s M_UNKNOWN: Room '!CNuYnsgsbanPKasUPz:matrix.org' does not exist`
`2017-03-15T20:24:17.370Z E M_UNKNOWN: Room '!CNuYnsgsbanPKasUPz:matrix.org' does not exist`
repeated forever. So it seems that ldbco.de had lost a particular room and it was somehow causing a sync failure. The room in question was a PM between `@lb:matrix.org` and `@lb:ldbco.de`.

This log shows how Riot rendered the room on the `@lb:matrix.org` account - shown here as 'luke'. 
```
Thu Mar 09 2017
luke joined the room.
luke made future room history visible to all room members.
luke invited luke (@lb:ldbco.de).
luke (@lb:ldbco.de) rejected the invitation.
luke invited luke (@lb:ldbco.de).
luke (@lb:ldbco.de) joined the room.
luke (@lb:ldbco.de) left the room.
luke invited luke (@lb:ldbco.de).
luke (@lb:ldbco.de) joined the room.
luke (@lb:ldbco.de) left the room.
luke invited luke (@lb:ldbco.de).
luke (@lb:ldbco.de) rejected the invitation.
luke invited luke (@lb:ldbco.de).

Fri Mar 10 2017
luke (@lb:ldbco.de) rejected the invitation.
```

Trying to view the same room from Riot logged in as `@lb:ldbco.de` results in the same `M_UNKNOWN: Room '!CNuYnsgsbanPKasUPz:matrix.org' does not exist`.

Somehow, this room was at some point being sent down the `/sync` response, only to stop existing later on, probably due to `@lb:ldbco.de` leaving it.
