/dev/ponies just reminded me that their server broke horribly a few months back with cryptic 4xx errors until we realised it was just ratelimiting traffic due to the federation RC limits being too low in the config.

Apparently their config is now:

> federation_rc_concurrent is up to 50 now and rc_reject_limit is up to 150, sleep delay decreased to 200, slimit limit upped to 50,

fwiw.

Is this also what's breaking OE?  Eitherway, we should presumably pick higher defaults, and/or fail more gracefully.
