`account_threepid_delegates.email` was removed in #13192 (v1.64.0rc1) but that was reverted in #13406 (v1.64.0rc2).

As part of the release announcement for 1.64.0 final, I would like us to give an unambiguous indication of when it will be removed.

Initially we had hoped to do this in 1.65, but [feedback](https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/pull/13406#issuecomment-1199115558) is that that is too soon.
