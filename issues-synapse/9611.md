**Description:**

It would be nice if the official docker image of synapse included [opencontainers-compatible labels](https://github.com/opencontainers/image-spec/blob/master/annotations.md). These can, for instance, be used by [renovate](https://github.com/renovatebot/renovate) to automatically fetch the changelog associated with new releases.
