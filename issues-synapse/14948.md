GraalVM is a polyglot VM, that offers the JVM languages together with a variety of other languages, who are implemented within the so called Truffle framework:

https://www.graalvm.org/python/

The performance characteristics seem promising, and considering the overwhelming use of matrix.org as a homeserver and its use of Synapse, I think this is an interesting option.

The project supports 3.8.5 at the moment, and I could see a cooperation be fruitful for both 😊
