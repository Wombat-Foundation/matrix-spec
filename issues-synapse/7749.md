* Matthew couldn't read me in matrix.to internal after I was invited.
* Ryan couldn't read Michael in room list internal after Michael was invited.
* Matthew can't read anoa in an internal room (presumably after being invited).

* matrix.org users can see EDUs (typing, receipts) but no messages
* the rooms are private

It's possible this is a nasty client regression, however it looks like a server-side issue given the localized impact.
