### Description

<!-- Describe here the problem that you are experiencing -->
When signing in on a new device, `notify_device_update` gets called immediately creating a massive burst of outgoing federation. This causes other unrelated issues like extremely slow initial sync or failed login (currently with FluffyChat) because the HS is stuck doing outgoing federation and killing the CPU.

### Steps to reproduce
- Workerless synapse with user that's federating with lots of HSs
- Login from a new device causing `notify_device_update` being called
- Watch Synapse flooding with outgoing federation

Would be nice if this could be deferred or slowed down enough not to cause such a huge issue immediately.
