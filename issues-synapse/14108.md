`filter_events_for_client` is time-consuming and expensive.

From this `/messages` request ([full trace](https://gist.github.com/MadLittleMods/c3ace249ce2aec77cb78d19a4f85a776)), it takes 7.83s for 500 events and is one of the biggest time saves left after other `/messages` optimizations we've already made. Most of time is just spent in the database getting the state out.
