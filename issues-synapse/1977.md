Currently, every time someone logs in, we create a device for them (to help them manage access_tokens, as well as for E2E crypto purposes).  This can cause services like matrix-ircd however to accumulate a tonne of devices, as every time someone signs into the ircd, the ircd validates their u/p and logs them in as a new device.  This is a problem in its own right (perhaps the ircd could cache access_tokens for mxids it's seen before), but the least we can do is to not spam federation with device_list updates for devices which aren't e2e capable.
