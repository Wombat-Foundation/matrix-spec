### Description

synapse ignores the app_name setting in homeserver.yaml
`homeserver.yaml
email:
   app_name: Matrix`

synapse/push/mailer.py:  
`self.app_name = app_name
...
logger.info("Created Mailer for app_name %s" % app_name)`

homeserver.log: - synapse.push.mailer - 91 - INFO - - Created Mailer for app_name Riot

### Steps to reproduce

- set app_name: Matrix in the email section of homeserver.yaml 
- activate email notification enable_notifs: True
- force a email notification
- check the Mail Header of the sent email

I get in every case [Riot] and not [Matrix] in the email subject of the notification email.

### Version information

- **Version**:        0.27.2-1
- **Install method**: apt-get install (deb https://matrix.org/packages/debian/ jessie main)
- **Platform**:  Debian 8.10
