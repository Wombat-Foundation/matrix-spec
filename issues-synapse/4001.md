It appears that prometheus_client 0.4.0 implicitly adds `_total` to the name of any Counter metric which doesn't have one, as per https://github.com/prometheus/client_python/releases/tag/v0.4.0 / https://github.com/prometheus/client_python/pull/300. Obviously, this will break all of our Counter metrics.
