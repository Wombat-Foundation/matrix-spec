**Description:**

<!-- Describe here the feature you are requesting. -->
At the moment, there are no "fallback" accounts on Matrix. So, if your current instance has an outage or other reasons that lead to it being nonoperative, then you'll have to wait until the instance is back up and/or will have to sign in to another account to temporarily chat without your chat history.

I'd like to have an option where you can setup fallback account(s). This makes it a lot more convenient and reliable for the end user because you will still be able to use your account and chat with others without being interrupted. At the moment, I use the `fedora.im` homeserver. If, for example, fedora.im has an outage, then it falls back to matrix.org or another desired instance, making me enjoy the platform without interruption.
