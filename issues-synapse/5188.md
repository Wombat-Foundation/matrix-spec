I would like to see the option to let your messages expire over time so servers are instructed to destroy the messages after expiry and more importantly that you can have a room where all messages expire over time. Sensible times to me could be a day, a week, a month or a year.

So two features:

1) be able to set messages in a room to expire.
2) be able to let your own messages expire.

Where expire means all federated servers are instructed to remove the message.
