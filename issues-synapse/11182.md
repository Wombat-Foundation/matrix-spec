**Description**
When 2 servers are federated, all users from the other server do not show up in the search results.

**Steps To Reproduce**
Federate 2 homeservers with each other and call /_matrix/client/r0/user_directory/search or search from your client.

Only some of the results from the federated homeserver show up in the list. List of results may increase overtime.

**Version Information**
Synapse 1.32.2
Installation Method: Debian/Ubuntu Package
