I'm sure this is a dup but I can't find the original, but it's still way too easy to end up with your client seeing a room with partial state.

Just had a great instance of this in HQ with brt on the elevate.at HS getting desktop notifs in Riot for a room he wasn't in.  Tapping on the notifs would show a peek into a room with 3 people, none of which were him.  Turns out this was a partial view of #test:matrix.org.  Looks like:

 * There was a state reset that pulled him back into #test:matrix.org
 * The state of #test:matrix.org was then partially synced with his HS
 * The HS synced the partial state to his Riot
 * Riot then failed to recover from this without clearing cache (probably https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/issues/2735?)

Surely state syncs should always be atomic to stop this sort of mess from happening?
