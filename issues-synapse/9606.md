### Description

When chatting concurrently in `#matrix:matrix.org` and other rooms, i noticed that my events werent sending for the other rooms (no `PDUs` for a very specific homeserver i was testing with)

My setup is 1 main process, 1 federation "sender" worker, and 1 federation + sync receiver.

Looking at the logs of my "sender" worker, the sender is processing bursts of send transactions, before tapering off while responses come in from slow servers, after which it bursts again.

I went onto a different account and observed `#matrix:matrix.org`, and messages of an hour ago were slowly trickling in.

My logs showed that the sender worker was sending small transactions (`EDUs: 1`, or `PDUs: 1`) to all servers in `#matrix:matrix.org`, while also not interleaving events to other rooms.

I note that typing events and read markers still go through, even though the slow federation.

I also need to note that i restarted my sender when i noticed it wasn't catching up, because i thought it had gotten stuck in a bug while working with the other homeserver creator, testing their homeserver.

### Steps to reproduce

- Use a main process + worker setup
- Join `#matrix:matrix.org`
- Spam (or have a very intense conversation)
- Observe sender log trying to catch up messages

### Version information

- **Version**: Synapse 1.29.0

- **Install method**: Docker

- **Platform**: Linux, Ubuntu 18

### Speculation

My hypothesis is that synapse fell behind with updates to matrix, and is now catching up again, blocking the other requests head-of-the-line-style.

My perceived fix onto this problem is to look at how synapse does federation-send queuing, and if it is based on a per-room-per-transaction model, then migrating it to a per-server model could fix this (and many other federation scaling problems), with batching into transactions as it sends and receives.

(This could optionally also allowing easy "do not immediately send" logic on presence, to then only include in the next transaction with "do send now"-esc events)
