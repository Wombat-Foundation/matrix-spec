### Description

It seems like you end up with a corrupt matrix HQ room: every few days, the room reappears in your room list, but not correctly: the room has no name then. It is listed with the roomname just "$some_username and 487 others" or such and the URL is just like `!cURbafjkfsMDVwdRDQ:matrix.org`  .  
You can then leave the room again, but after a few days it will reappear again and again.

### Steps to reproduce

- using **postgresql** use the [nuke-room-from-db.sh](https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/master/scripts-dev/nuke-room-from-db.sh) script from my PR https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/pull/2337 on the "Matrix HQ" room
- This seems to really delete the room, and it will first disappear from your room list in Riot
- wait a few days
