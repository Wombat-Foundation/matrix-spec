On quick discussion with @NegativeMjark, it sounds like there may be a way to just trust the perspectives servers to track the TLS certs of federated servers, rather than the server needing to know it itself.   This would be a major improvement in UX of federation, to avoid server admins have to mess around telling synapse what its own public cert fingerprint is (or providing infrastructure to help automate that).
