I am seeing user-interactive auth requests claim that the email stage is not complete even in the session that has just loaded riot-web having been redirected from clicking the email validation link.

```

https://matrix-client.matrix.org/_matrix/client/unstable/registration/email/submit_token?token=KmbokqMkWWlcidCNfSijQiDOrZiBOZuA&client_secret=[redacted]&sid=qBknnJfVRqgQPfrg

response headers:

content-type: text/html
date: Fri, 15 May 2020 12:03:55 GMT
location: http://localhost:8080/#/register?client_secret=[redacted]&hs_url=https%3A%2F%2Fmatrix.org&is_url=https%3A%2F%2Fvector.im&session_id=HVoLgwlhibZSGcEFeHfJNpDC&sid=qBknnJfVRqgQPfrg
status: 302

client then does a POST to https://matrix.org/_matrix/client/r0/register
request payload:
{"initial_device_display_name":"localhost:8080 (Chrome, macOS)","auth":{"session":"HVoLgwlhibZSGcEFeHfJNpDC"}}
```
