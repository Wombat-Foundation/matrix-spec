I bulk added 100 or so rooms to the IETF homeserver today (`trial1-matrix.ietf.org`) and found that after adding the rooms, they could not be searched for locally or externally. I can confirm that the endpoint I used to add them gave me a 200 OK. After joining the room manually via an alias (after leaving it), they then appeared in the listing.

Generally feels like the room directory cache is a bit too aggressive (or at least, certainly for smaller deployments) as I'd expect adding a new room to the directory would invalidate the cache. I've waited about 30 minutes and the rooms have yet to appear.
