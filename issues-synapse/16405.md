### Description

The [Grafana dashboard](https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/master/contrib/grafana/synapse.json) contains a mishmash of at least three different datasource UIDs for the Prometheus datasource type, leading to errors or "missing data" because only one of them is actually defined as a variable.

For example:
- `"uid": "000000001"`
- `"uid": "${DS_PROMETHEUS}"`
- `"uid": "$datasource"`
