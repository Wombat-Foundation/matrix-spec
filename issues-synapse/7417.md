https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/issues/7399 and https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/issues/7416 both look like a different unique constraint suddenly disappeared from their database (respectively `account_data_uniqueness` and `device_uniqueness`).

This doesn't seem to be related to sqlite -> postgres migrations, and both these constraints are added during table creation, outside of a background update, so it's not clear to me how this could happen.
