Currently the background update does at least 100 rows at a time, which means we handle 100 rooms at a time. Given we process the largest rooms first, and each one take a long time to process, this effectively means that the usual rate limiting applied by the background update manager doesn't have an effect.

This may cause unacceptable database or CPU usage on upgrade, or it may not. Currently matrix.org is running with a patch to lower the minimum and default to 1.
