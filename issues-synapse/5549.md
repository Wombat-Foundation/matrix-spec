For some reason, federation doesn't seem to happen with `matrix.org`. No-one on that homeserver can be reached, and no-one on it can reach my homeserver— but every other homeserver (disroot, ponies.im, etc.) seems to work fine. It looks like this has been going on since before the upgrade to 1.0.

The only section of my log that I thought looked relevant is here:

`2019-06-24 05:03:51,215 - synapse.crypto.keyring - 603 - WARNING - PUT-1829 - Key lookup failed from 'matrix.org': Failed to connect to remote server`

That's the only failure I could find in relation to matrix.org— and there are some successful HTTP requests:

`2019-06-24 05:19:36,702 - synapse.http.client - 298 - INFO - phone_stats_home-0 - Received response to PUT https://matrix.org/report-usage-stats/push: 200`
