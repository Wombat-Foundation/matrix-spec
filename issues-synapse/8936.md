### Description

<!-- Describe here the problem that you are experiencing -->
Missing history for rooms I'm rejoining

### Steps to reproduce

- Join an active room with a user on your homeserver. (As the only user from your homeserver in that room!).
- Stay in the room for a few days to gather some history.
- Leave the room.
- After a few days/a week, rejoin the room.
- Notice how your server feeds you the last events it had in the room before you left it.
It also feeds you new events since you rejoined the room.
However, all events during the time you were not in the room will not be fetched and fed to you by your homeserver.
You will see something like the following:

...
some message
some other message
more messages
you leaving the room
*everything that happend here is missing*
you joining the room (a week later)
new messages since you rejoined
...
