After registering in the home space, the user of this domain space has the right to create a space. This results in the server administrator being unaware of these spaces and what is going on there. There may be a situation for the distribution of good content. The second point is that the server administrator does not see and cannot delete already created spaces. I simulated a situation where a space was created, the space name was taken, and the administrator left all the rooms and the space itself. So I have a few questions:

1) how to forbid to create spaces?
2) how to view all created spaces on your server?
3) how to remove spaces ?
4) how to delete spaces and rooms created by other users. ?
