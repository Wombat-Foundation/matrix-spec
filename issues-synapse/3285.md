### Description

During an initial sync of my massive account, I saw this appearing as synapse was marking /sync requests as processed (25 minutes later).
```
2018-05-24 20:04:04,376 - synapse.rest.client.v2_alpha.sync - 348 - WARNING - GET-80775- Event '$15038575302050923rSTmh:matrix.org' is under room u'!cURbafjkfsMDVwdRDQ:matrix.org' instead of '!liSMaPmhIAVOyCHhdT:matrix.org'

2018-05-24 20:04:04,382 - synapse.rest.client.v2_alpha.sync - 348 - WARNING - GET-80775- Event '$15018848482433138yGYPS:matrix.org' is under room u'!cURbafjkfsMDVwdRDQ:matrix.org' instead of '!ZjJkQNQAlmRlGyyPSZ:matrix.org'
```
