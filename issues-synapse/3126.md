### Description

Someone sent me a direct chat invite and I cannot accept or reject it

### Detailed explanation

- Someone sent me a direct chat invite
- That person is from a homeserver (different from mine) which is currently down
- On Riot, I click on the invite, then click on "accept": I get a "Failed to join room" message saying "No known servers"
- On Riot, I click on the invite, then click on "reject": I get a "Failed to reject invite" message saying "Not a known room"
- On Riot, I click on the "..." to the right of the invite, then click on "reject" and "OK": I get a "Failed to reject invitation" message saying "M_UNKNOWN: Not a known room"

### Version information

- **Platform**:       virtual private server running debian jesse
- **Install method**: package manager
- **Version**:        0.27.4
