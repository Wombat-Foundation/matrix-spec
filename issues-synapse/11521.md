when you purge a room (via the [delete room api](https://matrix-org.github.io/synapse/develop/admin_api/rooms.html#delete-room-api)), we do not clear the in-memory caches. Then, when you rejoin, we have a bunch of the events in the cache, so do not bother to re-persist them.

This leads to very bad brokenness, like `state_groups_state` referring to events which do not exist.

(A workaround is of course to restart synapse after purging and before rejoining)
