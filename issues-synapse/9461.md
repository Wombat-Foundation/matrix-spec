The labeling scheme described in #9460 is focused on very coarse-grained categorization of Synapse bugs, primarily for use within Synapse itself.

Increasingly often, work in Synapse is part of larger, cross-repo efforts. For example, improving social login required coordination of work by Element's "User Delight" project team across many repos including Synapse, the Matrix Spec, and all Element clients.

While an ad hoc label (say, __Z-Delight-Social__ or __Z-Social-Login__) could tie together issues for that initiative writ large, @jryans has convincingly suggested that we could all also benefit from Area labels such as __A-Docs__ or __A-SSO__ to further narrow down related issues.

Should we adopt Area labels, and if so, which areas would make sense for Synapse?

cc: @jryans + @richvdh + @nadonomy for input.
