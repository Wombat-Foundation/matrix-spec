The background update to add a unique index to `e2e_cross_signing_keys.stream_id` (introduced in #8694) fails on some servers due to duplicated stream IDs.

This shouldn't be causing any issues as its just a background job that is failing, and I don't think we don't rely on uniqueness, but it's not ideal. (The reason for adding the index was to speed up start time)

It's not immediately clear to me how that has happened, since we should only be inserting one row per stream ID:

https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/1eb9de90c0dbb99a28304a295a0f6ebb0b8d1c6c/synapse/storage/databases/main/end_to_end_keys.py#L955-L963
