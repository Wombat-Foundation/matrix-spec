We have currently locked mypy 0.950. Since then, [0.960](https://mypy-lang.blogspot.com/2022/05/mypy-0960-released.html) and [0.971](https://mypy-lang.blogspot.com/2022/07/mypy-0971-released.html) have been released. I am particularly interested by ParamSpec-related fixes and improvements to the stdlib stubs in typeshed.

Now that a sufficiently new version of mypy-zope is available, we should upgrade mypy and mypy-zope to take advantage of the latest improvements.
