MSC3244 proposes adding a `room_capabilities` key to the response to [`/capabilities`](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.2/client-server-api/#get_matrixclientv3capabilities).

During development, `org.matrix.msc3244.room_capabilities` is to be used as an unstable placeholder.

Unfortunately, Synapse exposes this key *by default*, even in stable environments. We should disable this.
