`POST https://matrix.org/_matrix/client/api/v1/login
Content-Type: application/json
{"user":"@test:matrix.org","password":"ßßßßßß","type":"m.login.password"}`

`POST https://matrix.org/_matrix/client/api/v1/login
Content-Type: application/json
{"user":"@test:matrix.org","password":"éééééé","type":"m.login.password"}`

Both receive the following response. Various other characters are affected.

`{
    "errcode": "M_UNKNOWN",
    "error": "Internal server error"
}`
