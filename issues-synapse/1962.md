the device_list_stream table is supposed to store deltas of device lists (ie, just the devices which change), but sometimes it gets confused and ends up storing the entire device list for certain users.

We have users who have over 6000 devices (which is a separate problem), so it's possible to churn through disk space at gigabytes per hour.
