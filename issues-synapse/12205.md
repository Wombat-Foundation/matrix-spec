Above is a screenshot of what users see today after tapping 'Sign In With Apple' (SIWA) in iOS. According to [Apple's guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple/overview/data-management/), we need to make explicit the data is required vs an optional request.

- [ ] When using SIWA, update the headline copy
         from 'Your account is nearly ready'
         to 'Choose your account name'
- [ ] When using SIWA, update the byline copy
         from 'Check your details before creating an account on server'
         to 'This is required to create your account, and you can't change this later.'
- [ ] When using SIWA, prefill the username with whatever Apple has passed us
