This causes Synapse to log e.g. `Updating 'users_set_deactivated_flag'. Updated 100 items in 4ms.` even though it processed a single row.

That's because [`_background_update_set_deactivated_flag`](https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/blob/30af161/synapse/storage/registration.py#L816) returns the `batch_size` instead of `rows_processed_nb`.
