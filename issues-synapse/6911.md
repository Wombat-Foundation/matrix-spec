**Description:**

It'd be nice if there was a setting that allowed for moderation/approval of registration. Simply having either a "no registrations" or "open registrations" policy seems a bit binary. If enabled, a queue of users that have registered, and verified their e-mail, could be placed in some sort of holding queue until approved/denied by an administrator.

(And I'm assuming users that have not verified their e-mail address within nn time are already being automatically purged, otherwise, that'd be nice too.)
