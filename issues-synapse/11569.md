There are various methods in `DatabasePool` (such as `simple_insert_many_values`) which claim to take arbitrary `Iterable`s as arguments when in fact they cannot be generators (since they may be evaluated more than once: see https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/pull/11564).

We should change their definitions to `Collection`.
