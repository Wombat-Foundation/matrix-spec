App service users are excluded when generating event_push_actions: this is presumably missing a check for whether the app service user is exclusive, as it makes sense for users claimed exclusively by an app service to not get notifications (arguably) but an app service can be interested in events for normal users, so if you send, say, all events on your HS to an appservice, it will break notifs for everyone.
