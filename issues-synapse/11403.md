**Description:**

<!-- Describe here the feature you are requesting. -->
While room administrators can specify the privilege level of other participants (admin/moderator/etc), they have no control over invitation of new members (other than the ability to remove them after they join). There should be options to restrict this, especially in private encrypted rooms. There may be usage cases where it is desirable for historical chat data to be available to trusted new participants, but not to participants invited by an ignorant existing member. In such cases a malicious source could socially engineer a room member into sending them an invite and grab the whole chat before the admin deletes them.

Requested options:

Restrict inviting capability to room owner only
Restrict inviting capability to users with given access level [drop-down list: admin/moderator/etc]
Require invites to be approved unanimously by all members
Require session verification to be unanimous

This options should be available for private rooms, possibly also for public encrypted rooms (topic for discussion)
