BSD readlink does not have the `~~f`-flag. demo/start.dh fails with `readlink: illegal option -~~ f` on line 11.

As a workaround: You can remove the `-f`-flag and have a worse `$PYTHONPATH`, or, if you have `brew`, you can install GNU `coreutils` and use `greadlink` instead.

I'm not sure what is a good fix.
