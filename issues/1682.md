Several projects use our changelog and add checkboxes in front of each item to track their support for that release. We can probably make this easier with a `https://spec.matrix.org/v1.9/checklist.md` sort of thing.

Examples:
* https://github.com/matrix-org/synapse/issues/16709
* https://github.com/ruma/ruma/issues/1626

I'd suggest we go with doing something similar to Ruma's template - individual projects can edit the template further if they choose (like how Synapse does it).
