Because of all the spam, many people have started rejecting invites from unrecognised accounts.
That means that if someone is given someone else's account, she cannot simply get into contact, even if in real life they would recognise each other with joy - there is no way to say "Hi, it is me!"

I propose that is be possible to send a first message along with an invitation, which the recipient can read **before** deciding whether to accept or reject the invitation the recipient can read it.  The sender will receive no information about this unless and until the recipient decides to accept the invitation.

The message can simply be the first message in the room.
