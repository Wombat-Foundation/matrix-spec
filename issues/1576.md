At the moment only [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_TCP_and_UDP_port_numbers) has 8090, 8008 and 8448 listed.

If you look at the [Service Name and Transport Protocol Port Number Registry](https://www.iana.org/assignments/service-names-port-numbers/service-names-port-numbers.xhtml) it only shows
 * **8446-8449** as [Unassigned](https://www.iana.org/assignments/service-names-port-numbers/service-names-port-numbers.xhtml?&page=111)
* **8090** as [opsmessaging](https://www.iana.org/assignments/service-names-port-numbers/service-names-port-numbers.xhtml?&page=109)
* **8008** as [http-alt](https://www.iana.org/assignments/service-names-port-numbers/service-names-port-numbers.xhtml?&page=108)

The suggestion Would be to get 8448 or the whole range, depending on future needs ...

**It would be good to catch this Port officially so others cant.**
(*Service names are assigned on a first-come, first-served process, as documented in [RFC6335].*)
