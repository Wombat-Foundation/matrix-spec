**Issue**

Implementation guide makes no real mention of what the Postgres "chain cover index" is equivalent to in Rust-land, C++, or any other high-performance language that could achieve similar performance in memory (for a fraction of the storage cost).

This has led, in practice, to inefficient Rust implementations that bottleneck under large evaluations.
