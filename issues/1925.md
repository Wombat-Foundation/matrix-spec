In addition to voice and video calling, it would be very useful to have text calling, in which one could see what the others are typing, *in real time*.

It should use the same protocol(s) as voice/video calling, but should instead transfer text, rather than voice.

This would be a groundbreaking feature, and would close the gap between simple messaging and voice calling.
