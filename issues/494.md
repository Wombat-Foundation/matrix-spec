The [auth rules](https://matrix.org/docs/spec/rooms/v1#authorization-rules) say: 'If there is no previous m.room.power_levels event in the room, allow'. But the [C-S API](https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/latest#m-room-power-levels) says: 'If the room contains no m.room.power_levels event, the room's creator has a power level of 100, and all other users have a power level of 0' - and makes no exception for power_levels. This is, at best, confusing.

My assumption, working from the C-S spec had been that power-levels above 100 were impossible. We should figure out if PLs above 100 are a supported thing.
