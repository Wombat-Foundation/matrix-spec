**Suggestion**

In style familiar from Atheme IRC services (`$CHANACS`), I would like to have naive assignment of power level to a room ID.

For example I would like to give power level 50 to `!modbotcontrol:example.net` in `#help:example.net` which would then grant everyone who has access or is currently joined to `!modbotcontrol:example.net` the ability to perform power level 50 actions in `#help:example.net`. I imagine that would have to be verified by homeservers that are in both rooms with equal or greater powerlevel than the one assigned in `#help:example.net`.

This would let me have a single room to manage moderators instead of having to manage all rooms separately or I could grant a custom power level to users in trusted invite-only rooms.
