We currently have QR code cross-signing. You will typically perform the cross-signing immediately after you login on a new device.

Discord currently allows users to login with QR codes if they are already logged in on another device.

It seems like the obvious next step in terms of better UX is to kill two birds with one stone. I suggest that there be an option to login and cross-sign a new device simultaneously with one QR code.

In 99% of cases wherein you are verifying a new device, you will have just recently logged in anyway, so why not make it simpler & quicker?
