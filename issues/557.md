The [discovery](https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/r0.5.0#well-known-uri) process via `.well-known` includes checking `/_matrix/identity/api/v1` on the identity server, but we are planning to eventually disable v1 APIs from NV-controlled identity servers.
