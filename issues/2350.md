Element Web (and Classic mobile apps) use `org.matrix.preview_urls` as a cross account setting to enable/disable showing a preview of a URL under a message. I think this has been around since at least 2017 based on issues, but I think @ara4n may have implemented it even earlier.

I also *suspect* some clients in the ecosystem also make use of it, which is why I've put it in this repository rather than a bug against Element.
