**Suggestion**
I believe it would be extremely powerful to establish specific specifications for developing native widgets for clients such as Nheko or Fluffychat. Users of native clients typically do not appreciate the presence of web widgets. Many people might assume that crafting native widgets is exceedingly difficult, but it is achievable, especially if we were to have a Lua specification, for instance.
