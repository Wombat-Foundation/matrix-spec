**Link to problem area**: https://spec.matrix.org/v1.7/client-server-api/#room-previews

**Issue**

The spec mentions "peeking" several times in the C-S API, but it never really defines what peeking is. https://spec.matrix.org/v1.7/client-server-api/#room-previews mentions "lurking" but not peeking.

I suggest adding the text in bold:

> It is sometimes desirable to offer a preview of a room, where a user can “lurk” and read messages posted to the room, without joining the room. **This behaviour is sometimes referred to as "peeking"**.
