More of a potential spec bug, but in [/createRoom](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.6/client-server-api/#post_matrixclientv3createroom) we describe ``state_key`` as being optional and if omitted, it should be empty string. This might not be the best approach and in the future we should require it (this came up in matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals#1329).
