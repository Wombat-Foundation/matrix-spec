**Suggestion**

Sometimes it would be nice to have a room on loud but muted on a certain device. Currently this is not possible.

The specific usecase for me is that since there is no nice multi account support in element clients I have my work rooms on my Main account. However I dont want these chats to give me push notifications on my phone as usually it means I am out of Office anyway. It would be nice to keep them active at the PC but not on my phone therefor.
