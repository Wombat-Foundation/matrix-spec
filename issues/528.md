Link: https://spec.matrix.org/v1.13/client-server-api/#get_matrixclientv3directoryroomroomalias

It currently says 'servers that are aware of the room alias': no servers are going to be aware of it except the directory server, the others are meant to resolve it. It should say resident servers instead.
