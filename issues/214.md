https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/unstable#get-matrix-client-r0-rooms-roomid-initialsync says that `/rooms/{roomId}/initialSync` is deprecated, but it is in fact still used for [peeking](https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/unstable#room-previews). Until we figure out what to replace them with (#213), we should clarify this.
