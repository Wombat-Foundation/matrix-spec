Referring to the `StrippedState` schema under https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/r0.6.1#get-matrix-client-r0-sync

This allows clients to receive information about a room (its name, avatar, etc) after being invited to a room, but before actually *joining* (and receiving the full state) of the room. This effectively translates into the client being able to show the room's name and avatar in an pending invites list.

The `sender` field doesn't offer any practical benefit, and could be considered a minor data leak. I'm opting to remove it, though am curious to hear how clients may handle this, and whether this complicates logic for storing events in a client (even though these events already have most of this keys stripped anyways).
