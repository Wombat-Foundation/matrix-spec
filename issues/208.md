You end up reading a wall of your own messages talking to yourself.
Perhaps there should be option to limit self-redacting ability for certain room/person.
Also, I wouldn't erase every trace of redacted message in the timeline, preserving the entry instead with indication that it was redacted (or redacted by sender, if applicable).
That would maintain some level of consistency in rooms with problematic participants.
