**Suggestion**

On IRC I can use asterisk in banmasks to e.g. ban `@spam*:matrix.org` to ban all matrix.org users whose name begins with `spam`. This is commonly done together with IP bans #55, but can sometimes be useful without.

The other side of this issue is ban excemptions, in case a wellbehaving user matches a wide ban, they can still be permitted in. I think this could be useful in scenarios such as banning `@cat:*` to ban the instances of Cat Distruptor and then placing an exception on the couple not-bot cats around.

Alternatively a community could ban `@*:matrix.org` and then add exceptions on known well-behaved users to have more flexibility than an ACL deny offers.
