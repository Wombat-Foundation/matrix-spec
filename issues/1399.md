**Suggestion**

If I understand correctly, currently the only way to opt-out of `@room` mentions is to disable all notifications for a room which isn't desirable. It's possible for a room to mention everone more often than fits individual preferences, so I think having an option to disable the notifications per-room would be desirable.

I asked about this at https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec/issues/1074#issuecomment-1375861862 where I learned that `@room` has its own push rule and understand that the issue of replies to `@room` triggering notifications is addressed by https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/3664. I also asked in spec room and [was informed that in Matrix it's preferable to open a duplicate rather than asking whether an issue exists.](https://matrix.to/#/%23matrix-spec%3Amatrix.org/%24fPZHJjWZEwUatxy4F_Dj6gi3YPdvLvOgTemyqd2Ae0E?via=pikaviestin.fi&via=libera.chat&via=0x1a8510f2.space&via=the-apothecary.club)

Other platforms with the feature of pinging everyone, such as Discord has the option to disable `@everyone` notifications per-room and I think Telegram also has option to disable notifications when moderator pinning a message selects "notify everyone".
