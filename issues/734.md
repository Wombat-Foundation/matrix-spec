We might want to either:

* Not show breadcrumbs when there's only one item.
* Show something like "You are here: ..." in front so it's not just a naked hyperlink.
* Keep the `/` on the end if it's just one item.

Or something of that nature to give context to what that actually is, especially since it'll likely be one of the first things people see :)
