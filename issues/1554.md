**Link to problem area**: https://spec.matrix.org/v1.7/client-server-api/#put_matrixmediav3uploadservernamemediaid

**Issue**
The spec claims that the `PUT /_matrix/media/v3/upload/{serverName}/{mediaId}` endpoint doesn't require authentication:

![image](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec/assets/720821/978784e3-498d-4907-ac93-4cd8776f2144)


**Expected behaviour**
[MSC2246](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/blob/main/proposals/2246-asynchronous-uploads.md#put-_matrixmediav3uploadservernamemediaid) claims that “[...] This endpoint requires auth”.
