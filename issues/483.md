It would be good to mention to potential developers what would be a good way to back-off dead/unresponsive servers over federation. In my experience there is a **wide** number of failures that should be handled in subtle ways. I have some concerns that the best advice currently is "follow what Synapse does" which is pretty counter to the goal of the spec.
