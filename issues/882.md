**Suggestion**

When I upload a media file, I would like to provide attribution as an independent field to it freestyle including author, source, license. This should also be usable for user and room avatars.

Example scenarios:

* User wishes to use an avatar made by [Cat Avatar Generator](https://www.peppercarrot.com/extras/html/2016_cat-generator/index.php) as their profile picture. They are licensed under CC-By license so currently their best option to follow the license would be to add that to their display name, which then is possibly ugly and accessibility issue.
* A room wishes to use a GPLv2 licensed picture as their room avatar. Currently they can only append to the room topic "Room avatar by Whoever under GPLv2 license: sourcelink", which can become confusing with bridges, especially the IRC bridge which would likely bridge the topic over while IRC users won't see the room avatar itself.

*I brainstormed the general idea with @Sorunome*
