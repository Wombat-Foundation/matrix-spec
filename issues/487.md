It is a constant source of confusion that the spec has no high-level consistent guide to its structure.  The closest is chapter 1 of the index page: https://matrix.org/docs/spec/#matrix-apis, except:

 * It's labelled "Matrix APIs" rather than a ToC
 * The Introduction page itself isn't really called out other than a link in some prose as a fundamental part of the spec
 * The Appendices are even more buried, other than as a link in some prose, to the extent that i'd forgotten that the page even existed.

I suggest we have a proper ToC section for the whole Spec, listing the top-level sections for each doc in a single place, so that people can survey the overall structure and jump to the bit they need rather than having to read small print to find appendices etc.  This could potentially replace or include the "here are the current API versions" section.
