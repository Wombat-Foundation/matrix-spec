Quite a few people will invite bots to rooms to start talking to them but won't wait for the bot to join. This is more problematic when a bot isn't working correctly and can't join the room for some reason.

Other clients don't have an invite stage for bots (and sometimes users) and it seems to be the general expectation amoung users. To simulate the same behaviour in today's landscape there will need to be spec changes (because other users can't arbitrarily say that someone joined a room).

The auto-join-without-invite approach should also be applied to group chats. UX-wise it may be best to present this as "TravisR added Giphy to the room".
