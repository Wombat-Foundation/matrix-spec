Many are reusable outside of the spec they are defined in, such as `M_BAD_JSON` and `M_FORBIDDEN`. Some are very tightly scoped, like `M_THREEPID_DENIED`, but may still be of use to define outside of one particular area.

Should build off of the suggestions in https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-doc/issues/603.
