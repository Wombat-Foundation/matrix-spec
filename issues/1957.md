As reported by @zecakeh in https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec/pull/1937#issuecomment-2383089818, the `canonifyURLs: true` setting appears to be deprecated and causes issues when used together with `--baseURL`. We should probably work on removing `canonifyURLs`.
