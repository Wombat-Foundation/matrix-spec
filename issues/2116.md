**Issue**

The deployed version of the v1.13 spec is the `spec-historical` artifact, but the spec doesn't say it's old. This issue was introduced in v1.13 and is a problem in v1.14 too (through local/manual verification of the artifact).

v1.12 is unaffected: https://spec.matrix.org/v1.12/
