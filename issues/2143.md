Lately, the practice of reserving MSC numbers has been picked up by different contributors to the spec.

The bot in #matrix-spec currently announces the creation of these PRs:

![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd22575d-be10-4cdb-b988-b9f01b0e8b75)

These "reserved PRs" are initially entirely empty, however, which makes the announcement slightly unhelpful. Worse still, there's no announcement by the bot when the PRs are actually filled with content later.

Being able to reserve the number seems helpful to be able to use it in early implementations. On the flip side, it feels a lot harder to discover and track these MSCs compared to normal proposals.

Some thoughts to improve the situation:

* Make the bot announce the transition from "MSC number reserved" to "initial MSC content published"
* Require reserved MSCs to at least have a title
* Disallow reserving MSC numbers entirely (I don't think this is an option)
