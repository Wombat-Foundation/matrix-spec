The [spec claims](https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/r0.6.1#predefined-rules):

> Homeservers can specify "server-default rules" which operate at a lower priority than "user-defined rules". 

However, unlike the other server-default `override` rules, the `.m.rule.master` rule actually applies at higher precedence than user-defined `override` rules. The spec doesn't mention this at all.
