**Link to problem area**: https://spec.matrix.org/v1.9/client-server-api/#post_matrixclientv3joinroomidoralias

**Issue**
The *Third-party Signed* object, used for redeeming a 3PID invite, contains a `signatures` field.
But it doesn't say who the signatures are supposed to be *from*.

It also doesn't say why the signatures are needed, which can help with understanding.
