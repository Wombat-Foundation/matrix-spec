https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/r0.6.1#conditions says: 
> `key` The dot-separated field of the event to match, e.g. `content.body`

Various standard event fields (notably those in https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-doc/pull/1849) have dots in their names. It's unclear how these are handled.
