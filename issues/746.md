For better or worse, we have two versions of the client-server `/invite` endpoint. In OpenAPI these are differentiated by a single space at the end of one of the endpoints, but the anchors in the generated spec do not seem to consider this. Ultimately this leads to clicking the TOC to get at the "Third Party Invites" module then scrolling into the join endpoint only to have the TOC scroll upwards to the "Joining Rooms" section - not where the endpoint actually is in the document. This also means that the anchors on the page to go to the respective versions of the endpoint don't work and instead the browser chooses the first one, typically.
