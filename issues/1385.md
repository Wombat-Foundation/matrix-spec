**Suggestion**
_What would you like to see in Matrix? If your idea is vaguely complete enough, we
recommend submitting [an MSC](https://matrix.org/docs/spec/proposals) instead._

This would be the ability for a server admin to suggest users to join a room, like an status or announcement room. The only difference between this and the autojoin feature is that a user can freely choose whether they want to join the room or not instead of forcing them (even though they can leave later). _This would essentially function like an invite, just that it's automatic, and that it's a private invite and nobody in the room is notified._

This is mostly for privacy improvement, since some people may not wish for other people to know of the existence of their account.
