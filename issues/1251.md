**Suggestion**
What would you like to see in Matrix? If your idea is vaguely complete enough, we
recommend submitting [an MSC](https://matrix.org/docs/spec/proposals) instead.

The timeout function should place an user on a 'timeout' when they're unable to speak (muted) for a specific period of time.
We could think of it as a 'temporary mute'. Users with a power level equal or higher to 50 (Moderator) should be able to do this.

We may also want to timeout a user on just a specific room or on an entire space.

Additionally, we want to show the muted user a message that states that they were muted with the reason and time left included, and the inability to add reactions to posts.
