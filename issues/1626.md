From [the Matrix specification](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.7/#timestamps):
> ### Timestamps
>
> Unless otherwise stated, timestamps are measured as milliseconds since the Unix epoch. Throughout the specification this may be referred to as POSIX, Unix, or just “time in milliseconds”.

**Issue**
This does not talk about leap seconds, or rather has two conflicting statements about them. The number of milliseconds since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 includes leap seconds, but [Unix time](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Unix_time&oldid=1168330527) usually does not. In Unix time, each day is fixed to have 86400 seconds.

How should the matrix spec be interpreted wrt. that? Can timestamps repeat (Unix time) or can they not (most implementations would probably be wrong since that's not Unix time anymore).
