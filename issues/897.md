The C-S spec refers to things like [`m.receipt`](https://spec.matrix.org/unstable/client-server-api/#mreceipt) and [`m.typing`](https://spec.matrix.org/unstable/client-server-api/#mtyping), and various bits of account data as "events". I think this is confusing - it gives the impression that such objects can appear in a room timeline, which is not the case.
