Starting an issue to capture some of the suggestions and considerations around better tooling to support work on and around the spec. There's a consensus that there's room for improvement. Noting that this is not an active project and ultimately requires scoping and resourcing to make it so, let's at least capture our thoughts to get ourselves that much closer to a world where we can implement improvements.

I invite folks to share things like pain points, user stories, feature requests, and other considerations. Let's refrain from implementation details though as that'd be putting the cart before the horse.
