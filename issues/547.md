For example, redacting a `m.room.member` event is the same regardless of whether it has a `state_key` or not.  There may be some possibility for abuse if clients don't properly distinguish between state and non-state events when displaying events.  We may also want to fix this under the goal of only retaining things that are absolutely necessary.
