**Link to problem area**:

https://spec.matrix.org/unstable/client-server-api/#forming-relationships-between-events

**Issue**

>  the `m.relates_to` key of an event MUST be included in the plaintext copy of the event

"plaintext" is simply wrong here: plaintext is stuff that was/will be encrypted; cleartext is stuff that is never encrypted; cf https://www.hypr.com/cleartext/. The `m.relates_to` property is in the part of the event that is never encrypted.

(while we're there: our [style guide](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec/blob/main/meta/documentation_style.rst) says 'avoid the term "key" unless you are specifically talking about the *name* of a property'. The text above is referring to the *property*, not the key.)
