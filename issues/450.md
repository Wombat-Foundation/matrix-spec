Something I noticed in the Steam UX is you can hand out a link to people so they can add you as a friend and you end up accepting. It looks like they have a limited number of uses as well, probably to prevent your friends list being filled with bots.

Would be interesting to see this concept brought to Matrix: a link you can hand someone and it automatically sets up a chat, etc to come talk to you. Would allow the sender to sign up however they want (email, phone, native account, etc) and still be able to reach you. 

Technically we do have `?action=chat` that we can stick on the end of matrix.to URIs, but I'm not sure this really fits the purpose. Opening this as a vague idea of something that might be useful.
