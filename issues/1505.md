https://spec.matrix.org/v1.6/client-server-api/#syncing
https://spec.matrix.org/v1.6/client-server-api/#post_matrixclientv3joinroomidoralias

**Issue**:
There are currently at least a few of the type names which collide with other in spec on the terms of style, `Third Party Signed`, `Invited Room`, `Joined Room` etc. which have spaces and others in the spec don't, like `StrippedStateEvent`, `ClientEventWithoutRoomID`, `UnsignedData`. This style, without spaces, represents the majority of type names in the spec currently. 

I think we should make them all in one style, with spaces or without them.
