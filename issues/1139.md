#### 1. feature-name
Matrix working with Bluetooth

#### 2. feature-description\what would you like to see in Matrix?
I would like Matrix to work with Bluetooth.

#### 3. Why?
1. This allows people to chat locally if the internet connection drops.  ***This can be a head start, as matrix is a protocol that works with interoperability.***
2. Unlike Wi-Fi, Bluetooth connections do not use up your cellular data package because they aren't transmitting data over the airwaves in the same way. Bluetooth is exclusively device-to-device transmission with no intermediaries. Bluetooth typically operates over short distances to conserve power. ***This can be a head start, as matrix is a protocol that works with interoperability.***
3. This can be something innovative, big apps like Wechat, Whatsapp, Telegram do not use this technology for this purpose.
4. I talked to some people in the matrix-hq chat about this idea.

#### 3.1 What is Bluetooth?
Bluetooth is a wireless technology that uses a radio frequency to share data over a short distance, eliminating the need for wires. You can use Bluetooth on your mobile device to share documents or to connect with other Bluetooth-enabled devices. For security reasons, Bluetooth devices must be paired before they can begin transferring information. The process of pairing your devices will vary depending on the device you are connecting to.
