[`GET /_matrix/client/v3/publicRooms`](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.10/client-server-api/#get_matrixclientv3publicrooms) claims to not require authentication, despite [Synapse requiring authentication by default](https://element-hq.github.io/synapse/latest/usage/configuration/config_documentation.html#allow_public_rooms_without_auth).

Note: [`POST /_matrix/client/v3/publicRooms`](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.10/client-server-api/#post_matrixclientv3publicrooms) on the other hand does require authentication.
