**Link to problem area**:
https://spec.matrix.org/unstable/client-server-api/#_matrixclientv3pushrules_pushcondition

**Issue**

![image](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec/assets/1389908/1068b711-bed2-406e-a87a-887d73e121e2)

It is not an array; rather it should be one of `string`, `integer`, `null` or `boolean`. In other parts of the spec (eg [`PushRule`](https://spec.matrix.org/unstable/client-server-api/#_matrixclientv3pushrules_pushrule)) we represent this with a `|` character. (Note that in the case of `actions`, it is *actually* an array of `string`s or `object`s, so the `[]` are correct in that case.)
