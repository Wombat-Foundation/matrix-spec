**Link to problem area**:

https://spec.matrix.org/v1.6/#identity, https://spec.matrix.org/v1.6/identity-service-api/, and everywhere.

**Issue**

The names "identity service" and "identity server" *repeatedly* cause confusion with people thinking they are responsible for managing user identity in Matrix, which is not the case at all.

(For clarity: all they do is provide a way of mapping from a ["third-party identifier"](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.6/appendices/#3pid-types) such as email address or phone number onto a Matrix User ID, to make it easier for your existing contacts to find you.)

I can't really think of a good name though. "Third-party identity mapping service" doesn't exactly trip off the tongue. Suggestions welcome.
