In the spec on the sync API https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/r0.6.0#id251 on presence updates, the following is specified:

"The updates to the presence status of **other users**." (Emphasis mine)

This would appear to suggest that the user's own presence is excluded, which at least to me appears to contradict https://matrix.org/docs/spec/client_server/r0.6.0#events-on-change-of-profile-information 

Perhaps this could use different wording to make the intent more clear?
